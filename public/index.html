How to play

The source sends energy upward. A splitter lets the beam continue straight and also sends a copy 90° to its left. Mirrors turn beams by 90°. Beams stop at targets and walls. Activate every target.

Day complete

Scoring

Your score is the total number of rotations across all five grids. Par is the fewest possible on each grid. Undo and reset put the board back but do not refund moves, and a finished grid is locked. 3 stars: par. 2 stars: up to 2 over. 1 star: 3 or more over.
